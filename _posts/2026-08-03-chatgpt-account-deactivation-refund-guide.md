# ChatGPT 封号退款焚诀

用被封号的邮箱给 [ar@openai.com](mailto:ar@openai.com) 发信（注意，是用被封账号本身的邮箱发）

申诉模板如下：

## 主题

```text
Full Refund Request – Deactivated ChatGPT Account – 你的邮箱
```

### 邮件内容

```text
Dear OpenAI Appeals Team,

My ChatGPT account associated with 你的邮箱 has been deactivated, and I can no longer access the paid subscription.

I am no longer requesting restoration of access. Please cancel the subscription and issue a full refund of 金额及币种 to the original payment method.

Billing details

Case number
你的 Case

Charge date
扣款时间

Amount
金额及币种

Receipt number
收据编号

Order number
订单号

Card
卡品牌及尾号

The invoice and receipt are attached.

Please confirm the full refund to the original payment method.

Sincerely,

你的名字
```

附件放**付款收据**、**发票**和**登录报错截图**。

卡只写品牌和后四位。

邮箱密码、验证码、完整卡号、CVV、Cookie、Session，这些东西一律别发。

客服会让你在 Approve 和 Deny 里面选一个。

确定退款的话，就在原 Case 邮件内回复一次

```text
Approve. Please issue the full refund to the original payment method. Thank you.
```
